About AfyaTech

Born in Nairobi.
Built for Africa.

AfyaTech started with one question: what if every community health worker in Kenya had a brilliant clinical partner available 24/7 — in their own language?

Our Story

A Healthcare Crisis That Demanded a New Answer

Kenya faces one of the world's most severe healthcare worker shortages: a ratio of 1 doctor for every 16,000 patients. With 70% of the population living in rural areas far from hospitals, millions of Kenyans navigate complex health decisions with little professional guidance.

AfyaTech was founded in 2017 by a team of Kenyan clinicians, engineers, and public health experts who believed artificial intelligence — deployed ethically and trained on local data — could change this equation fundamentally.

We started with a simple SMS-based clinical assistant for CHWs in Kisumu. Today we support over 8,000 health workers across Kenya, with an agent network spanning intake, triage, clinical support, and long-term follow-up.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Five principles that guide every product decision, every model deployment, and every community engagement AfyaTech undertakes.

Patient First

Every feature, every model choice, every governance policy starts with one question: does this improve patient outcomes? If the answer is uncertain, we wait.

Radical Equity

We audit AI performance across gender, geography, and socioeconomic group. If our tools work better for urban patients than rural ones, we fix it before we ship.

Transparency

Patients always know when AI is involved in their care. Clinicians are never permitted to present AI output as their own independent judgment.

Data Sovereignty

Patient data is stored on Kenya-domiciled servers. We do not sell or share patient data. Patients can download or delete their full records at any time.

Human Oversight

AI provides decision support. A licensed clinician makes and owns the final decision — always. No automated high-stakes clinical action without human sign-off.

African-Built

Our models are trained on locally-sourced, ethically-consented Kenyan health data. We partner with county hospitals to build representative training corpora.

Our Journey

From Prototype to Platform

Six years of iteration, community listening, and clinical partnership — building toward a future where every Kenyan has access to quality healthcare guidance.

2017
Founded in Nairobi
SMS-based CHW assistant launched in Kisumu County with 50 health workers.
2019
AI Clinical Documentation
First SOAP note generation AI goes live, reducing documentation time by 60%.
2021
Multilingual Platform
Swahili and Kikuyu language models integrated. Patient education reach triples.
2023
Triage Agent Pilot
WHO IMCI-based triage AI deployed in 5 counties with real-time clinician alerts.
2025
Full Agent Network
Five-agent orchestration pipeline integrating intake, triage, clinical support, CHW field support, and patient follow-up.
2027
National Scale
Target: all 47 counties covered. AfyaTech AI Ethics Charter published. Contributing to Kenya National AI in Health Policy.
