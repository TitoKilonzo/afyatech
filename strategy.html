Terrain 1 · Savannah

Precision Prompting for Clinical Workflows

The Savannah terrain established that AI output quality is a direct function of prompt quality. In healthcare, the stakes are higher than any other domain — a poorly prompted AI can suggest an incorrect treatment or omit a critical warning.

AfyaTech's prompting strategy is built on four principles: role clarity, hard constraints, format specification, and few-shot anchoring.

Role Assignment

Every clinical prompt opens with a precise role that establishes clinical boundaries before any instruction.

Hard Constraints

Explicit "Do NOT" instructions for diagnosis, prescriptions, and uncertain outputs — default to escalation.

Format Specification

SOAP notes, triage JSON, patient education — each has a precise output structure the prompt explicitly enforces.

Few-Shot Anchoring

2–3 gold-standard examples included for rare presentations and locally specific East African disease patterns.

Template 1 — CHW Clinical Note Generation

Converts raw CHW voice or text intake notes into structured SOAP-format clinical records.

Prompt Template · Clinical Documentation
You are a clinical documentation assistant for AfyaTech. Convert the CHW's raw intake notes into a structured SOAP note. CONTEXT: The CHW operates in a rural Kenyan dispensary. The patient has consented to AI-assisted documentation. RULES: - Generate ONLY the SOAP note structure. Do NOT diagnose. - Flag any symptom meeting WHO danger sign criteria with ⚠️ - If the patient is a child under 5, apply IMCI framework headings. - Output in English. Translate any Swahili phrases accurately. - End every Assessment section with: "Requires clinician review." FORMAT: S (Subjective): [Patient's reported complaints, history] O (Objective): [Vitals and observable findings as provided] A (Assessment): [Summary only — no diagnoses. Requires clinician review.] P (Plan): [CHW actions taken. Escalation reason if applicable.] INPUT NOTE: {chw_intake_note}

Template 2 — Patient Triage Prioritisation

Assigns urgency levels 1–5 and routes patients to appropriate care pathways.

Prompt Template · Triage Agent
You are a triage support system for AfyaTech. Assign a triage level and routing decision. TRIAGE SCALE: 1 = Immediate (life-threatening) → Emergency referral now 2 = Urgent (risk of deterioration) → Clinic within 2 hours 3 = Semi-urgent → Clinic within 24 hours 4 = Non-urgent → CHW follow-up within 72 hours 5 = Self-care → Patient education provided MANDATORY LEVEL 1 OR 2 FLAGS: - Difficulty breathing in a child under 5 - Chest pain with diaphoresis - Altered or reduced consciousness - Active seizures - Signs of obstetric emergency OUTPUT — respond ONLY with this JSON, nothing else: {"level": <1–5>, "routing": "<destination>", "reason": "<one sentence>", "flags": [<danger signs if any>]} PATIENT SUMMARY: {patient_summary}

Template 3 — Multilingual Patient Education

Prompt Template · Patient Education
You are a patient health educator for AfyaTech, Kenya. Generate a plain-language explanation of the health topic below for a patient with no medical background. REQUIREMENTS: - Reading level: Standard 6 (approximately age 12) - Language: {language} (Swahili / English / Kikuyu) - Length: 150–200 words. Tone: Warm, respectful, non-alarming - Include: What it is · What to expect · One actionable step - Define any medical term immediately after using it - Final line must be: "Please ask your health worker if you have questions." TOPIC: {health_topic} PATIENT CONTEXT: {age}, {gender}, {relevant_condition}

Prompt Quality Deployment Checklist

Role clearly defined with scope of practice limits stated
Negative constraints included (Do NOT diagnose, Do NOT prescribe)
Output format explicitly specified (SOAP, JSON, prose)
Escalation pathway defined for uncertain or high-risk cases
Tested on minimum 20 real CHW case notes
Clinician sign-off received before deployment

Terrain 2 · Tsavo

AI Fluency — Building Organisational Intelligence

The Tsavo terrain revealed that AI tools are only as powerful as the team using them. AfyaTech's clinical and operational staff must understand not just how to use AI, but how it works, where it fails, and when to override it.

AI fluency is a prerequisite for safe healthcare deployment — not an optional extra.

AI Capability Map for AfyaTech

What AI Can Do Well
Clinical documentation Symptom summarisation Drug interaction lookup Patient education Appointment scheduling Swahili ↔ English ICD-10 code suggestions Follow-up drafts Literature search CHW training support
What AI Cannot Do — Hard Limits
Physical examination Final diagnosis Prescribe medication Real-time lab results Empathetic counselling Legal/insurance decisions Guarantee accuracy Replace clinical judgment

Critical Concepts Every AfyaTech Staff Member Must Know

Hallucination

LLMs can generate plausible-sounding but entirely fabricated information — inventing drug names, dosages, or statistics. All AI clinical output must be verified against an approved source before being acted upon. No exceptions.

Knowledge Cutoffs

AI models cannot access real-time drug updates, outbreak data, or revised clinical guidelines. AfyaTech connects to a live Kenya Essential Medicines List API to supplement base model knowledge.

Confidence ≠ Accuracy

AI presents fabricated and correct information in the same confident tone. CHWs are trained to treat every AI output as a first draft that always requires review — never as authoritative guidance.

Terrain 3 · Ethical Savannah

Ethics & Governance — Responsible AI in Healthcare

Healthcare AI without ethics is harm at scale. The Ethical Savannah challenged AfyaTech to ask: who benefits, who is harmed, who decides, and who is accountable?

These principles are embedded into every AI decision — from model selection to patient communication.

Legal & Regulatory Framework

Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

Patient health data is sensitive personal data under Kenyan law. AfyaTech requires explicit opt-in consent, data minimisation, and rights to access and deletion. A Data Protection Officer is appointed and registered with the ODPC.

Kenya Health Act & KMPDC

AI operates strictly as clinical decision support. Diagnosis and prescription remain the legal mandate of licensed practitioners. AfyaTech has engaged KMPDC and the MOH for regulatory guidance and compliance dialogue.

The Five Ethical Pillars

01
Equity — AI That Works for Everyone
AfyaTech audits model performance across geographic, socioeconomic, age, and gender groups. Any performance gap greater than 5% between subgroups triggers a mandatory retraining review. Skin condition AI receives heightened scrutiny given historical underperformance on darker skin tones.
02
Transparency — Patients Deserve to Know
Patients are always informed when AI is involved in their care — in plain language, in their preferred language. The AfyaTech app displays a clear "AI-assisted" indicator. Clinicians may never present AI output as their own independent clinical judgment.
03
Human Oversight — The Final Word Stays Human
AfyaTech operates a strict human-in-the-loop policy for all clinical decisions. AI provides decision support; a licensed clinician makes and owns the final decision. Automated high-stakes actions require clinician confirmation before execution.
04
Data Sovereignty — Patient Data Belongs to Patients
All patient health data is stored on Kenya-domiciled servers. AfyaTech does not sell or share data with third parties. Anonymised aggregate data may be used for model improvement only with community consent.
05
Accountability — Someone Is Always Responsible
Every AI output in a clinical pathway has a named responsible human. AfyaTech maintains an AI incident register: any near-miss or adverse event linked to AI output is reviewed by the Ethics Board within 14 days, with findings published in the annual Transparency Report.

Risk Tiering Framework

Low
Administrative and Educational AI
Appointment reminders, health tips, insurance form pre-fill. Automated deployment permitted after template-level clinician approval. Monthly audit cycle.
Medium
Clinical Documentation and Triage Support
SOAP note generation, triage scoring, drug lookup. CHW can view output; a clinician must confirm before acting. Weekly audit cycle.
High
Diagnostic Support and Treatment Recommendations
Differential diagnosis suggestions, dosage calculations, referral decisions. Licensed clinician review mandatory before any patient contact. Daily audit cycle.

Terrain 4 · Agentic Pride

Agent Orchestration — AI That Acts, Not Just Answers

The Agentic Pride terrain moved beyond single-turn AI into coordinated multi-agent systems. For AfyaTech, this means AI agents working together across the full patient journey — extending clinician capacity without replacing clinician judgment.

Five specialised agents, each with defined risk tiers and human checkpoints, coordinate to deliver seamless, safe clinical support.

The AfyaTech Agent Network

Agent 1 — Patient Intake
Low risk · Fully automated
Greets patients via WhatsApp, USSD, or the AfyaTech app. Collects demographics, chief complaint, symptom duration, and hands off a clean structured summary to the Triage Agent.
WhatsApp USSD Translation
Agent 2 — Clinical Triage
Medium risk · Clinician SMS for Level 1–2
Applies WHO IMCI and adult triage algorithms to assign a 1–5 urgency score. Flags danger signs. Immediately notifies the duty clinician for all Level 1 and Level 2 assignments.
SMS alerts Facility locator
Agent 3 — Clinical Decision Support
High risk · Human review always mandatory
Prepares pre-consultation clinical briefs for clinicians only. AI-suggested differentials are clearly labelled. Never communicates directly with patients.
Kenya Formulary MOH Guidelines
Agent 4 — CHW Field Support
Medium risk · All queries supervisor-logged
Mobile-first AI mentor in Swahili and English. Answers protocol questions and locates referral facilities. Defaults to "not sure — refer" for all ambiguous clinical queries.
Offline capable CHW protocols
Agent 5 — Patient Follow-Up
Low risk · Escalates to Triage if needed
Sends post-visit follow-ups, medication reminders, and checks in on chronic disease patients. Auto-escalates to the Triage Agent if a patient reports worsening symptoms.
WhatsApp/SMS Health tracker

Orchestration Flow with Human Checkpoints

Patient contacts AfyaTech via WhatsApp, USSD, or app
Agent 1 · Intake — structured patient history collected
Agent 2 · Triage — urgency level 1–5 assigned
Human checkpoint — Level 1 or 2: immediate duty clinician notification via SMS
Agent 3 · Clinical Support — pre-consultation brief for clinician
Human checkpoint — clinician reviews, consults patient, owns all final decisions
Agent 5 · Follow-up — chronic disease monitoring begins
If worsening symptoms reported → escalate back to Agent 2
