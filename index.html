Our Mission

Bridging Africa's Healthcare Gap with Responsible AI

AfyaTech is a Nairobi-based healthtech startup building AI tools that give community health workers, nurses, and doctors a powerful clinical partner — one that is always available, multilingual, and trained on East African health data.

From rural dispensaries in Kisumu to urban clinics in Nairobi, our platform translates complex clinical data into actionable insights — in Swahili, English, or Kikuyu — while always keeping the human clinician at the center of every decision.

What We Do

AI Solutions for Every Clinical Touchpoint

From patient intake to long-term follow-up, AfyaTech's agent network supports the full healthcare journey.

Clinical Documentation AI

Converts CHW voice and text intake notes into structured SOAP-format clinical records, reducing documentation time by up to 70%.

Learn more

Intelligent Triage

Assigns urgency levels 1–5 using WHO IMCI protocols and routes patients to the right care pathway with instant clinician alerts for emergencies.

Learn more

Multilingual Patient Education

Generates plain-language health education in Swahili, English, and Kikuyu — calibrated to Grade 6 reading level for maximum patient comprehension.

Learn more

Clinical Decision Support

Prepares pre-consultation briefs with suggested differentials, drug interaction flags, and relevant Kenya clinical guidelines — for clinician review only.

Learn more

Patient Follow-Up Agent

Sends personalised reminders, monitors chronic disease patients, and escalates worsening symptoms back to the triage pipeline automatically.

Learn more

CHW Field Support

A mobile-first agent that answers CHW protocol questions in Swahili, locates nearest referral facilities, and logs every interaction for supervisor review.

Learn more

Ethics First

AI That Is Safe, Equitable, and Accountable

In healthcare, AI without ethics is harm at scale. Every AfyaTech product is governed by our Five Ethical Pillars: Equity, Transparency, Human Oversight, Data Sovereignty, and Accountability.

Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 compliant
Explicit patient consent, data minimisation, right to deletion
Quarterly bias audits across demographic subgroups
By county, gender, age — results published in our annual Transparency Report
Human-in-the-loop for every clinical decision
