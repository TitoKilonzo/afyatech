Our Services

AI Tools Built for Clinical Reality

Six integrated AI products, designed for the constraints and opportunities of East African healthcare — low connectivity, multilingual patients, and overstretched clinicians.

Medium Risk · CHW Review Required

Service 01

Clinical Documentation AI

Converts raw CHW voice and text intake notes into fully structured SOAP-format clinical records — in English or Swahili — reducing documentation time by up to 70%.

  • Automatic IMCI framework for children under 5
  • WHO danger sign flags marked with visual alerts
  • Swahili phrase translation with clinical accuracy
  • ICD-10 code suggestions for structured records
  • EHR integration via HL7 FHIR API
Voice input Mobile-first Offline capable WhatsApp
Medium Risk · Clinician Notified Levels 1–2

Service 02

Intelligent Triage Agent

Assigns urgency levels 1–5 using WHO IMCI protocols and adult triage algorithms, routes patients to the appropriate care pathway, and instantly notifies the duty clinician via SMS for all Level 1 and 2 assignments.

  • WHO IMCI and adult triage algorithms combined
  • Real-time SMS and push notification for emergencies
  • Structured JSON output for EHR integration
  • Nearest facility routing via geolocation API
  • Audit log of every triage decision
SMS alerts Facility locator WHO guidelines DB
Low Risk · Clinician Template Approved

Service 03

Multilingual Patient Education

Generates personalised, plain-language health education in Swahili, English, or Kikuyu — calibrated to Grade 6 reading level with age and condition-specific context for every patient.

  • 3 languages: Swahili, English, Kikuyu
  • Grade 6 reading level for maximum comprehension
  • Condition-specific templates (malaria, TB, diabetes, hypertension)
  • Delivered via WhatsApp, SMS, or printed handout
  • Clinician-approved template library
WhatsApp delivery Print-ready 3 languages
High Risk · Clinician Review Mandatory

Service 04

Clinical Decision Support

Prepares a pre-consultation clinical brief for clinicians: patient history, AI-suggested differentials (clearly labelled), drug interaction flags, and applicable Kenya clinical guidelines. Never communicates directly with patients.

  • Kenya Essential Medicines List API integration
  • Drug-drug and drug-condition interaction flags
  • AI differentials clearly labelled — never presented as diagnoses
  • Kenya MOH clinical guideline references linked
  • PubMed literature search for rare presentations
Kenya Formulary API MOH guidelines PubMed
Medium Risk · All Queries Supervisor-Logged

Service 05

CHW Field Support Agent

A mobile-first AI agent accessible via the AfyaTech CHW app. Answers protocol questions in Swahili and English, locates nearest referral facilities, and defaults to "not sure — refer" for all ambiguous clinical queries.

  • Available 24/7 in Swahili and English
  • Nearest facility locator with referral letter generator
  • Every query logged for supervisor review
  • Offline mode for low-connectivity areas
  • Safe default: escalation for all ambiguous queries
Offline capable Facility locator CHW protocols
Low Risk · Automated with Clinician Templates

Service 06

Patient Follow-Up Agent

Sends personalised post-visit follow-up messages, medication reminders, and appointment confirmations. Monitors chronic disease patients at clinician-defined intervals and escalates worsening symptoms back to triage automatically.

  • TB, diabetes, and hypertension chronic disease monitoring
  • Medication adherence reminders via WhatsApp or SMS
  • Auto-escalation to triage on symptom worsening
  • All message templates clinician-pre-approved
  • Health outcomes tracking dashboard for clinicians
WhatsApp/SMS Outcomes tracker Scheduler API
